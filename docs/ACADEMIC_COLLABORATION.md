# Academic & Collaboration Module
**Module Contributor**: Diya Ponda (Member 2)

### Implemented Components:
- **Academic Companion & Assignments**: Assignment tracker, deadlines, submission management, and progress tracking.
- **Campus Intel**: Campus notices, academic updates, resource sharing, and announcements.
- **Study Match & Study Groups**: Peer study matching, group creation, and collaborative sessions.
- **Team Builder**: Hackathon and project team formation with skill-based matchmaking.
- **Local Persistence (Room Database & DAOs)**: Room database schema and Data Access Objects for assignments, study groups, teams, and intelligence posts.
